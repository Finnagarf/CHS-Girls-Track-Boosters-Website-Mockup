Who we are

We are the booster club for the girls track and field program at Champaign Central High School. We are a nonprofit organization led by parents and community members. We support the team by raising funds to cover the cost of uniforms, equipment, recognition, and events.

Why the program matters

Central's Girls track is open to all students with no required cost or try-outs for prospective athletes. The team relies on community support in order to run track meets, organize events, and purchase the equipment and resources athletes need.
