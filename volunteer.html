How you can help

Students, parents, family members, and community members are all welcome for volunteering. Volunteers may be asked to help with:

  • Track meets

    Helping the team's home meets run smoothly.

  • Concessions

    Staffing the concession stand, which helps fund the program.

  • Fundraising events

    Supporting events such as car washes and online fundraisers.

  • Booster activities

    Helping with the banquet, team meals, and other events.
