Why this program matters

The Champaign Central Girls Track Team is an important organization for the community. it provides students an accessible way to be part of an athletic team. There is no participation fee, so no student is kept out because they can't afford to play. It is especially important the team has no barriers to entry, because over 44% of the athletes are considered low-income. (Source: Illinois Report Card, Central High School, 2025, "Low-Income" students (opens in a new tab).)

High school athletics provides a positive environment with many benefical features, including:

  • Opportunities for college recruitment and scholarships
  • A sense of community and belonging for athletes
  • Mentorship from coaches and older teammates
  • Personal growth, self-confidence, and leadership building

Research has found that participation in sports is associated with better academic performance in adolescents. Source: Nguri, M. (2024). Effects of Sports Participation on Academic Performance in Adolescents. American Journal of Recreation and Sports, 3(1), 12–23. doi.org/10.47672/ajrs.2048 (opens in a new tab)

How we raise and spend funds

In a typical year, The boosters raise about $8,000 and spend about $6,000 on the team's ongoing needs. All figures are approximate.

Where funds come from

  • Booster club membership fees
  • Concession sales at meets
  • Community fundraisers, such as a car wash and online fundraisers

Where funds go

  • Team uniforms
  • Everyday equipment and gear
  • Recognition: banquet, awards, state shirts, and team meals

Why we keep a reserve

Left over funds from each year get carried over to the next. We aim to keep a balance of at least $2,500 for unexpected needs. For example, we recently bought replacement hurdles and digital timing equipment, which was very costly.

Who benefits

  • Athletes

    Athletes get the equipment, uniforms, and recognition they need to train and compete at the best of their ability.

  • Families

    With no participation fee, families don't need to find the money for their daughters to be able to join the team.

  • The school

    A strongly supported team adds to the athletic community at Champaign Central High School, and positively impacts the students involved.

  • The community

    Local supporters and businesses help students in their own community succeed.
