  • May 13, 2026 · Meet Recap

    Five Central Athletes Qualify for State at O'Fallon Sectional

    Central finished 13th as a team at the O'Fallon Sectional with 19.5 points. Audrey Allender (pole vault), Izzy Roundtree (100m), and Londyn Grant, Izzy Roundtree, Ava Clark, and Sarah Donahoe (4x400 relay) qualified for the 2026 IHSA State Meet. Overall, the team recorded twelve personal bests during the meet.
